# Example output

`zotac-rtx3060ti-repair/`: one complete run, 2026-11-06, on the two-server reference lab (docs/reference-lab).
The question was board-level repair of a ZOTAC RTX 3060 Ti Twin Edge OC LHR that artifacts and crashes worse and
worse even underclocked, with "leave no stone unturned". 3 rounds, 41 subagents, 12 fact checks, 4 laptop passes,
7.8 hours.

- `plan.md`: the brief and the sub-questions (round 2–3 follow-ups appended by the critic)
- `verification.md`: the 12 fact-check verdicts (several corrected claims the earlier rounds had stated)
- `report.md`: the 14,560-word report, 169 cited sources

The notes, frames and downloaded files the report rests on stay out of this repo (they hold third-party material).
The report is reproduced as the swarm wrote it. It is a demonstration of the pipeline, not repair advice anyone
has checked.
